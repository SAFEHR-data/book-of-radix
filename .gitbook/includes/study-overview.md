---
title: Study Overview
---

The Inglés studies aims to improve the care of patients with acute infections by optimising antibiotic prescribing through a novel digital infrastructure. It comprises three sub-studies with related dependencies and overlapping aims, each delivering a different category of point-of-care information to prescribing clinicians at the moment of antibiotic prescribing:

* **Inglés-Guidelines**: can a digital nudge improve compliance with guideline-directed antibiotic prescribing?
* **Inglés-Resistance**: can surfacing a patient's historical antibiotic resistance profile reduce the prescription of antibiotics to which resistance has already been demonstrated?
* **Inglés-Estimated Susceptibility**: does presenting clinicians with patient-specific predictions of antibiotic susceptibility improve treatment decisions and patient outcomes?

**Inglés-Guidelines** evaluates a digital nudge to encourage guideline-concordant prescribing in patients receiving antibiotics for community-acquired pneumonia.

**Inglés-Resistance** evaluates the presentation of patient-specific microbiology and prescribing history to clinicians treating urinary tract and related infections. Where no prior resistance data exist and the selected antibiotic does not conform to guidelines, Inglés-Resistance uses the same infrastructure as Inglés-Guidelines to deliver a guideline reminder nudge.

**Inglés-Estimated Susceptibility** evaluates the use of a machine learning model — trained on clinical, microbiological, and prescribing data — to predict the likely effectiveness of a selected antibiotic. Clinicians are shown these predictions at the point of prescribing, and the study evaluates whether doing so improves antibiotic selection and patient outcomes.

This protocol describes **Inglés-Guidelines** (Layer 1).
