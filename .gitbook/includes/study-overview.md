## Study Overview

The primary aim of the Ingles study is to improve the care of patients with acute infections through the optimisation of antibiotic prescribing using a novel digital infrastructure and ecosystem.  The Ingles study is formed of three sub-studies with related dependencies and overlapping aims:

1. Ingles Uno - _can a digital nudge, delivered to prescribers at the moment of antibiotic prescribing improve compliance with guideline directed care?_
2. Ingles Dos - _can surfacing patient-specific historical microbiological data and previous antibiotic prescription data to prescribing clinicians at the moment of antibiotic prescribing reduce the prescription of antibiotics with established resistance data?_
3. Ingles Tres - _does presenting clinicians with patient-specific predictions of optimal antibiotic choice improve patient care?_

Ingles Uno evaluates a digital nudge for encouraging guideline compliant prescribing in patients receiving antibiotics for community acquired pneumonia.  Ingles Dos explores presenting patient specific microbiology and prescribing data to patients receiving antibiotics for urinary tract infection and related infections.  Where no prior microbiology data or prescription data exists and the selected antibiotic does not conform to existing guidelines, Ingles Dos will use the same infrastructure as Ingles Uno to deliver a nudge to encourage clinicians back to guideline adherent prescriptions.  Finally, Ingles Tres will evaluate the implementation of predictions from a machine learning model trained on clinical, microbiological and previous prescription data.  Clinicians will be shown predictions of the effectiveness of the selected antibiotic in order to evaluate whether this improves the delivery of antibiotic treatment and improves patient outcomes.  &#x20;
